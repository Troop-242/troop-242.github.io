## Regular Activities

### Camping

We plan monthly campouts to locations across the region. Scouts learn outdoor skills including fire building, orienteering, cooking, and Leave No Trace principles.

### Service Projects

Giving back to our community is a core part of scouting. Our troop organizes service projects throughout the year, from park cleanups to food drives.

### Merit Badges

Scouts work toward earning merit badges in a wide range of subjects -- from Wilderness Survival to Citizenship in the Community. We host regular merit badge workshops.

### Summer Camp

Each summer, our troop attends a week-long summer camp where scouts can earn merit badges, develop skills, and make lasting memories.

### High Adventure

For older scouts, we plan high adventure trips including backpacking, canoeing, and other challenging experiences.
