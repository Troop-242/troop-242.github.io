## Who Can Join?

Scouts BSA is open to young men and women ages 11 through 17. No prior scouting experience is required.

## How to Join

1. **Visit a meeting** -- Come see what we're all about. Guests are always welcome.
2. **Talk to our leaders** -- We'll answer any questions you and your family may have.
3. **Register** -- Complete the BSA registration and join the adventure.

## What to Expect

New scouts are welcomed into a patrol and paired with experienced scouts who help them get started. Within the first few months, most new scouts:

- Learn basic scout skills
- Go on their first campout
- Start working on rank advancement
- Make new friends

## Costs

Annual dues cover registration with the BSA, Boys' Life magazine, and troop supplies. Additional costs for camping trips and uniforms are kept affordable. Scholarships are available -- no scout is turned away for financial reasons.
