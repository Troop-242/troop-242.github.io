## Our Mission

Troop 242 prepares young people to make ethical and moral choices over their lifetimes by instilling in them the values of the Scout Oath and Scout Law.

## History

Troop 242 was the first Girl Troop in Virginia and has a proud tradition of developing young leaders in our community. Our scouts participate in camping trips, service projects, and merit badge workshops throughout the year.

## Leadership

Our troop is scout-led, with adult leaders providing guidance and mentorship. Scouts take on leadership roles within patrols and the troop, learning responsibility and teamwork.
