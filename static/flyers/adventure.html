Ready for a real adventure?

Pitch a tent in the snow. Navigate a 5-mile hike by compass alone. Find your way at night without any electronics. Build a fire from scratch and cook a meal over it. In Scouting, every rank brings a bigger adventure.

Camping Hiking Backpacking Climbing Wilderness Survival Canoeing Kayaking Whitewater Swimming Scuba Diving Cycling Snow Sports Orienteering Pioneering

Start with your first overnight campout as a Tenderfoot. By Second Class, you'll take a 5-mile compass hike and find directions by the stars. By First Class, you'll run an orienteering course, use GPS, and sleep in lean-tos and snow caves. Then come the high-adventure treks.
