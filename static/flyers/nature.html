Troop 242 Scouts BSA

Are you curious about nature?

From tracking wildlife to reading the weather by cloud formations, Scouts explore the natural world firsthand. You'll identify 10 kinds of wild animals by their tracks and signs, learn to spot poisonous plants, and read natural indicators of incoming storms — all while camping under the stars.

Nature Bird Study Mammal Study Forestry Environmental Science Reptile & Amphibian Study Insect Study Geology Astronomy Weather Oceanography Plant Science Soil & Water Conservation Fish & Wildlife Mgmt

By Second Class rank, you'll identify 10 wild animals in the field. By First Class, you'll know 10 native plants, read weather forecasts, and describe natural indicators of hazardous weather. Add merit badges in Forestry, Geology, or Oceanography to go even deeper.
