Troop 242 Scouts BSA

Like working with your hands?

Tie knots that hold real weight. Build a camp gadget from poles and lashings. Start a fire from tinder you prepared yourself, then cook a full meal over it. Sharpen a knife, split firewood, and make s'mores. Scouts learn by doing.

Cooking Camping Pioneering Woodwork Wood Carving Welding Metalwork Leatherwork Basketry Home Repairs Plumbing Electricity Farm Mechanics Architecture

At every rank, Scouts build real skills. You'll learn square knots, bowlines, and taut-line hitches — then use them. By First Class, you'll tie timber hitches, lash poles into structures, plan a menu, budget the groceries, and serve as head cook for your patrol on a campout.
