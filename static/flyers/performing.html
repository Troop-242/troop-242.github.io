Love the spotlight?

Whether you're drawn to the stage, the screen, or the mic, Scouting gives you real opportunities to perform — and the confidence to own it. Lead campfire programs, teach skills to fellow Scouts, and present to community leaders. Campfire skits are just the beginning.

Theater Music Bugling Moviemaking Public Speaking Communication Animation Journalism

At every rank, Scouts practice speaking in front of others — explaining the Scout Law, leading flag ceremonies, and teaching peers new skills. The Theater badge covers acting, directing, and stagecraft. Music and Bugling develop your instrument skills. And it all builds the confidence that carries into everything else you do.
