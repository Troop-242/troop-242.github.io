Could you save a life?

Treat a snakebite. Splint a sprained ankle. Rescue a swimmer in trouble. Transport someone from a smoke-filled room. Scouts train for real emergencies so they're ready when it matters — not someday, but now.

First Aid Emergency Preparedness Lifesaving Search & Rescue Safety Swimming Fire Safety Public Health Health Care Professions Crime Prevention

First aid training starts at Tenderfoot — treating cuts, burns, frostbite, and choking. By Second Class, you handle shock, heatstroke, and severe bleeding. By First Class, you learn CPR, bandaging, victim transport, and how to develop an emergency action plan for your home.
