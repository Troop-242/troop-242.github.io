Want to become a leader?

Scouting is one of the only youth programs where young people actually lead. Serve as patrol leader. Teach a younger Scout how to tie knots. Plan meals, budgets, and service projects. Visit elected officials to discuss citizenship. You won't just learn about leadership — you'll practice it every week.

By Star rank, you'll hold a leadership position for four months and log six hours of community service. By Life, you'll teach younger Scouts using the EDGE method and lead conservation projects. Eagle Scouts plan and execute a major service project that benefits the whole community.
